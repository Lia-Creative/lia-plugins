# Lia voice — the standard

The source of truth for how Lia copy should sound. `ai-patterns.md` detects how AI writes by default; this says what to write instead. Two layers: the **Lia base voice** (applies to all Lia copy) and **product-voice profiles** (a product layers its own vocabulary + rules on top). Apply the base plus whichever profile is in scope.

---

## The Lia base voice

Lia builds tools for Life in Adventure People. The company principles shape the copy:

- **AI invisible, outcomes in front.** Never market "AI-powered", "smart", "powered by". The copy describes what the tool *does for the person*, not the machinery. No prompts, no chat, no model-talk.
- **Calm, not clever.** Lia serves people like Jess — often neurodivergent, often overwhelmed, wanting one calm tool. Copy is uncluttered and legible under load. Clarity over cleverness; never a wall of text or a system to administer.
- **Plain over performed.** Write like a person who knows the thing, not someone performing thoughtfulness. Closer to a well-written README than a thought-leadership post. No jargon, no ceremony, no manufactured significance.
- **Trust is the product.** The voice earns trust by being straight, specific, and non-manipulative. No hype, no urgency, no engagement-bait, no "jamming" (the category's push-it-down-your-throat default is exactly what Lia rejects).
- **Practical, brave, collaborative, built to last.** Concrete and useful; honest about limits; warm without being sentimental.
- **Australian English**, Macquarie spelling. Real Australian specifics where they ground a claim (myGov, Medicare, the ATO, council collection nights) beat generic ones.

### The moves that make it Lia
1. **Show, don't announce.** Create the feeling with a specific, true detail; never tell the reader how to feel.
2. **Trust the reader.** End on a plain statement or a concrete next step, not a conclusion drawn for them.
3. **Precision is warmth.** The care shows in getting the specific right, not in adjectives about care.
4. **One clear line beats three punchy ones.** Direct means clear, not clipped.

## Product profile — Held

Held is the couples household-roles tool. Its voice is the Lia base, tuned warmer and more intimate, plus a fixed vocabulary. Governed by `Products/Playground/Held/Discovery/held-point-of-view-2026-07-06.md` (the signed-off POV).

**Held vocabulary (use exactly; never the Fair Play terms):**

| Concept | Held word | Never |
|---|---|---|
| The unit of responsibility | **a hold** | card, task, chore, role |
| The grouping | **a sphere** | suit, domain, category |
| The invisible→visible arc | **Notice · Plan · Do** | CPE, conception/planning/execution |
| The agreed bar | **what "done" looks like** | Minimum Standard of Care |
| Protected personal time | **your own thing** | Unicorn Space |
| Reassigning | **the hand-off** / **the monthly reset** | the deal / re-deal |
| The forward view | **the horizon** | — |

The six spheres: **The Table · The Nest · The Engine Room · The People · The Two of You · The Horizon.**

**Held voice rules:**
- **Second person, both-sided, gender-neutral.** Written to a couple; both partners find themselves in it. Never "the primary parent", never gendered defaults.
- **Lead with the invisible.** A hold's "done" opens at Notice — the noticing is the job.
- **Felt cost, not time cost — and only where it earns it.** Name what a hold *takes to carry* ("the low hum of always tracking X") rather than minutes. Use it on the genuinely heavy invisible holds; don't append it to every entry.
- **No scorekeeping, ever.** No counts, percentages, balance, "fair share", or tally language. Load is shown as ownership + felt strain, never a score.
- **Warm, but not sentimental.** The warmth is in the accurate, recognisable detail (the 5pm "what's for dinner?" dread), not in poignant closers about what it all means.

## Quality checks

Qualitative reads, not pattern matches. Pass/fail each with a one-line note. If a check fails, propose the fix in the report; don't apply silently on a soft pass.

1. **AI invisible / outcomes in front.** Does the copy sell what the tool *does for the person*, or does it talk about "smart"/"AI"/the machinery? Fail = reframe to the outcome.
2. **Calm and legible.** Could Jess read it under load without bouncing? Fail = cut, simplify, shorten.
3. **Emotion created, not broadcast.** Is there phrasing that tells the reader how to feel ("what's striking…", "it's real work")? Fail = cut it; let the specific detail carry it.
4. **The reader is trusted.** Does it end on a plain statement / concrete step, or conclude the significance for the reader? Fail = cut the closer.
5. **Plain, not performed.** Does it read like someone who knows the thing, or someone performing thoughtfulness/poignancy? Fail = flatten.
6. **No manufactured significance.** Any sentence whose only job is "this matters"? Fail = cut, strengthen the observation instead.
7. **Specific and true.** Are the specifics real (sourced), and Australian where relevant? Fail = flag fabricated detail; swap generic for AU-real.
8. **No hype / no jam.** Any urgency, superlatives, engagement-bait, or push? Fail = cut; trust the reader.
9. **(Profile) Vocabulary held.** For a product profile, are the product's own terms used and the banned ones absent (Held: no card/suit/CPE/MSC/Unicorn Space)?
10. **(Held) Both-sided and scorekeeping-free.** Written to both partners; no tally/balance language anywhere?
